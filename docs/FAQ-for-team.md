# Questions for the SKO team

We are building a "Common questions" page for the website. These are the questions donors, funders
and the general public actually ask — and the ones Google and AI assistants get asked about
organisations like SKO.

**Eleven are already answered** from material SKO has supplied, and are live on the site now.
**Nine need an answer from the team.** Please review both lists.

---

## Part 1 — already answered. Please check these are correct.

These are on the website now. If any answer is wrong, say so and we will change it.

1. **Does SKO run an orphanage or residential care centre?** → No. SKO's work is to prevent children
   being separated from their families in the first place.
2. **Is SKO a religious organisation?** → No. Local, neutral, non-sectarian, non-political,
   non-racial, non-profit, registered with the Ministry of Interior.
3. **What does SKO do?** → Works with the most vulnerable families in Phnom Penh's urban poor
   communities; home visits, needs identification, referrals to services.
4. **Who does SKO help?** → Families in poverty in Phnom Penh, and the children and young people in
   them.
5. **What is the Family Development Approach?** → A structured method built on the premise that
   families are not short of capability but of support, information and access.
6. **How long does SKO work with a family?** → No fixed length. Assessment, then weekly or
   twice-monthly home visits, then phase-out, then re-assessment six months later.
7. **What happens after SKO stops working with a family?** → Re-assessment six months after
   phase-out.
8. **Why does SKO work in people's homes rather than at a centre?** → Because the obstacles are in
   the home, and travel cost is often why support never starts.
9. **How does SKO measure its results?** → Cases managed in OSCaR.
10. **Why does this website not show photographs of children?** → A child's safety outweighs a good
    photograph.
11. **How can my organisation partner with SKO?** → Email info@samatapheapkhnom.org.

Also already on the site: **Is SKO registered?**, **Where does SKO work?**, **What does Samatapheap
Khnom mean?**, and three donation questions.

## Part 2 — we need answers from the team

Please answer in **English and Khmer** where you can. Two or three sentences each is right — each
answer has to make sense on its own, because search engines and AI assistants quote them without the
rest of the page.

### Safeguarding — highest priority

**Q1. How does someone report a concern about a child?**
We need: the named person or role who receives it, the direct email or phone number, and what happens
after a report is made. *This is the most important question on the website.*

**Q2. Can donors, partners or visitors come and meet the children?**
We need SKO's actual policy. Our suggested position, for you to confirm or correct: programmes are
delivered in families' own homes, SKO does not arrange visits to meet children, and partners are
welcome to meet the team instead. Does SKO ever host funder or partner site visits, and under what
conditions?

**Q3. Can foreigners volunteer with SKO?**
What is the policy? Does it differ for roles involving contact with children? This is a
high-traffic question in Cambodia and a clear answer builds trust.

### Donations

**Q4. Is a donation to SKO tax deductible?**
For Cambodian donors, and for donors overseas. If the answer is no, or it depends, say so plainly.

**Q5. Can people donate goods instead of money?**
School materials, hygiene kits, bicycles and food all appear in SKO's programmes. Are in-kind
donations accepted? If yes, what is useful and how does someone arrange it? If no, say so.

**Q6. Can a donor sponsor a specific child or family?**
Our expectation is no — support goes where the assessment shows it is needed, and publishing details
about individual children would breach safeguarding. Please confirm.

**Q7. How is a donation actually spent?**
The percentage split between programmes, staff and training, and administration — from the most
recent audited accounts. Funders look for this.

### Organisation

**Q8. Who governs SKO?**
We have Executive Director Samnang Moun. Is there a Board of Directors, and how would you like it
described?

**Q9. Who funds SKO?**
Which donors, ministries or partners can be named publicly? Some funders require acknowledgement;
others prefer not to be listed. We will only publish what you approve.
