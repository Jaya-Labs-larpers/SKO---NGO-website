# Editing the SKO website — a guide for staff

You do not need any technical knowledge to use this. If you can use Facebook or Word, you can do
everything in this guide.

**ខ្មែរ:** [HANDOVER-km.md](HANDOVER-km.md)

---

## The two places

| | What it is | Address |
|---|---|---|
| **The website** | What the public sees | `https://<your-domain>` |
| **The Studio** | Where you write and edit | `https://sko.sanity.studio` |

You only ever work in **the Studio**. You never touch the website directly.

## Logging in

1. Go to `https://sko.sanity.studio`
2. Click **Sign in with Google** (or whichever method you were invited with)
3. Use the email address SKO invited you with

If it says you do not have access, ask whoever manages the Sanity account to invite you. Do not
create a new account — it will not be connected to SKO's content.

---

## Both languages, always

Nearly every box you fill in appears **twice**: once marked **English** and once marked
**ខ្មែរ (Khmer)**.

**The website does not translate anything for you.** When a visitor clicks the language button, they
see exactly what you typed in that language's box. If you leave the Khmer box empty, Khmer visitors
see the English text instead — the page still works, but it is not in Khmer.

So: **fill in both boxes every time.** Write the English, then write the Khmer. They do not have to
be word-for-word — write what reads best in each language.

## Writing a news or activity post

This is the thing you will do most often.

1. In the left-hand menu, click **✍️ Activities & news**
2. Click the **pencil-and-paper icon** at the top to start a new one
3. Fill in:

   | Box | What to put |
   |---|---|
   | **Title** | The headline, in both languages |
   | **URL slug** | Click **Generate**. Leave it in English letters — this becomes the web address |
   | **Date** | Today's date is filled in already; change it if the event was earlier |
   | **Category** | News, Event, Story or Announcement |
   | **Cover image** | The main photo (see the photo rules below) |
   | **Summary** | One or two sentences. This is what shows on the list page |
   | **Body** | The full article |
   | **Related program** | Optional — links the post to Family Care, Child Protection or GBV Prevention |
   | **Show on the homepage** | Turn on for important posts. The three most recent appear on the front page |

4. Click **Publish** (bottom right)

The post appears on the website in **two to three minutes**. Refresh the page if you do not see it
straight away.

### Editing a post you already published

Open it, change what you need, click **Publish** again. The old version is replaced.

### Removing a post

Open it, click the **three dots** next to Publish, choose **Delete**. It disappears from the website
at the next update.

## Adding an annual report, financial statement or policy

1. Click **📄 Reports & policies** → new document
2. Fill in the **Title** (both languages) and the **Year**
3. Choose the **Type**: Annual report, Financial statement, Policy, or Other
4. Upload the **PDF (English)**
5. If you have a Khmer version, upload it under **PDF (Khmer)** — Khmer visitors will then get the
   Khmer file automatically
6. **Publish**

The website shows the file size next to each download. Many of our visitors are on mobile data, so
**please compress large scanned PDFs before uploading.** A 20 MB scan is a real cost to someone on a
phone.

## Updating the Director's message

1. Click **👤 Director's message**
2. Change any of: the portrait photo, the name, the title, the pull quote (the large sentence), or
   the message itself
3. **Publish**

There is only ever one of these — you are editing the existing one, not making a new one.

---

## Changing the numbers on the website

Every figure on the site — families supported, children reached, percentages — is editable.

1. Click **📊 Impact statistics**
2. Open the one you want to change
3. **Value** is the plain number, used for sorting
4. **Display value** is what visitors actually see. Use this if you want `10,000+` or `96%` or Khmer
   numerals `៩៥%`
5. **Context line** is the small text underneath, like "since 2007"
6. **Publish**

> **Please always fill in the context line.** A number on its own reads like advertising. A number
> with "since 2007, across 18 communes" underneath reads like evidence — and the people deciding
> whether to fund SKO know the difference.

## Photographs and safeguarding

This part matters more than anything else in this guide.

- **Never publish a photograph that identifies a child**, unless you have documented consent and the
  photo is genuinely safe to publish. If you are unsure, do not use it.
- **Never use a beneficiary's real name.** In the Stories section, use a pseudonym or initials. The
  field says so, and it is not a suggestion.
- Stories have a **Consent confirmed** switch. A story **will not appear on the website** until it is
  turned on. Only turn it on when documented consent actually exists.
- If you have a good story but no photo you can safely publish, turn on **Use an illustrated avatar**.
  The website draws a simple silhouette instead. This is the normal, expected choice — not a
  second-best one.
- **Every image needs alt text** — a sentence describing what is in the picture, for people who
  cannot see it. The Studio will not let you save without it.

For the organisational chart, the alt text should describe the **reporting lines in words** — "the
Board oversees the Executive Director, who oversees…" — not just say "organisational chart". Someone
using a screen reader cannot see the picture at all.

## When does the website update?

Every time you click **Publish**, the website rebuilds automatically. It takes **two to three
minutes**.

The website also rebuilds itself every night. This is how job posts disappear on their own once
their closing date has passed — you do not need to delete them.

---

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The Studio will not let me publish | Look for a red message on a field. Usually a required box is empty, or a story's consent switch is off |
| I published but the website has not changed | Wait three minutes and refresh. Try a hard refresh: **Ctrl+Shift+R** |
| Still not showing after ten minutes | The automatic rebuild may have failed. Contact whoever manages the Cloudflare account |
| I deleted something by accident | Open the document, click the **three dots** → **Review changes**. Sanity keeps a full history and you can restore an earlier version |
| The website shows `[PLACEHOLDER]` somewhere | That field has not been filled in with real content yet. Find it in the Studio and replace it |

---

## What you cannot change here

These need a developer:

- The design — colours, fonts, page layout
- Which pages exist, and the menu structure
- The contact form fields and where the messages go
- Anything about how the site is hosted

Everything else — all the words, photos, numbers, PDFs, staff, partners, jobs — is yours.
